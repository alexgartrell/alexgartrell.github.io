Why this works

It is easier to count the rooms with no match. The second person avoids the first birthday with chance 364/365, the third avoids both with 363/365, and so on, so P(n) = 1 − 365 × 364 × … × (365 − n + 1) ÷ 365ⁿ. At 23 people that crosses 50%, and at 57 it passes 99%. With 366 a match is guaranteed.

It feels paradoxical because we picture our own birthday being matched. But a match can come from any pair, and n people make n(n − 1) / 2 pairs. 23 people make 253 pairs, each with a 1 in 365 chance. This assumes all 365 days are equally likely and ignores February 29; real birthdays bunch up a little, which makes matches slightly more likely.
