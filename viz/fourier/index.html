How it works

Treat each point of the drawing as a complex number x + iy, and walk around the shape as time t goes from 0 to 1. The path is first resampled to 256 evenly spaced points, so a slow stroke doesn’t count more than a fast one. The discrete Fourier transform then writes those points exactly as a sum of terms ck e2πikt.

Each term is a circle: |ck| is its radius, the angle of ck is where it starts, and k is how many turns it makes per loop (negative turns run clockwise). Stack the circles tip to tail, biggest first, and the last tip draws the shape. Keeping only the biggest few gives the best smooth approximation. The energy figure is Σ|ck|² for the circles used, as a share of the total.
