Visualizations

Little interactive experiments with math and randomness. Everything runs in your browser.

Probability & randomness

  • Darts for π → Monte Carlo · Probability

    Throw random darts at a circle inside a square, then count the misses to estimate π.

  • Buffon’s needle → Monte Carlo · Geometry

    Drop needles on a floor of parallel lines and count how many cross one to estimate π.

  • Galton board → Probability · Central limit theorem

    Balls bounce through rows of pegs and pile into bins that trace the binomial and normal curves.

  • The birthday paradox → Probability · Combinatorics

    Fill a room one person at a time and watch shared birthdays appear much sooner than intuition says.

  • Monty Hall → Game · Probability

    Pick a door, watch the host reveal a goat, then stay or switch. Simulate thousands of games to see why switching wins 2 in 3.

Chaos & patterns

  • The chaos game → Fractals · Randomness

    Jump halfway to a random corner of a triangle, again and again, and the Sierpinski triangle draws itself.

  • Double pendulum → Chaos · Physics

    Release a fan of double pendulums a millionth of a radian apart and watch them swing as one, then fly apart.

  • Mandelbrot explorer → Fractals · Complex numbers

    Click to zoom into the Mandelbrot set, visit famous spots, and preview the Julia set under your cursor.

  • Game of Life → Cellular automata · Emergence

    Draw cells or load a glider gun, then watch four simple rules produce gliders, oscillators, and chaos.

Algorithms

  • Sorting race → Algorithms · Complexity

    Six sorting algorithms race side by side on the same shuffled bars, counting every compare and write.

  • Pathfinding → Graph search · Algorithms

    Draw walls and mud, drag the start and goal, and watch BFS, Dijkstra, A* and greedy best-first race to the goal.

  • Fourier epicycles → Fourier series · Signals

    Draw a closed shape and watch a chain of spinning circles, biggest first, trace it back one term at a time.
