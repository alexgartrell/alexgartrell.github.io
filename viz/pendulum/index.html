How it works

Each pendulum follows the exact equations of motion from Lagrangian mechanics: two 1 kg bobs on weightless 1 m rods, no friction. They are stepped forward 1,000 times per simulated second with the fourth-order Runge–Kutta method, whose error shrinks like the step size to the fourth power. Without friction, total energy should never change, so the energy drift is an honest check on the integrator.

The motion is chaotic: a tiny difference in the start grows roughly exponentially, multiplying by e every 1 / λ seconds, where λ is the Lyapunov exponent. That is why the gap rises as a straight line on the log chart until it reaches the size of the pendulum. Making ε 10× smaller only buys about ln 10 / λ more seconds of agreement.
