Why switching wins

Your first pick is right with probability 1 / 3, and nothing the host does can change that, because he can always find a goat to show you. So staying wins 1 / 3 of the time.

Switching wins exactly when your first pick was wrong, which happens 2 / 3 of the time. The host knows where the car is and must avoid it, so the door he leaves shut soaks up all of that probability. With n doors, staying wins 1 / n and switching wins (n − 1) / n. Try 100 doors.
