Games

A little time well wasted. Single-page games that run entirely in your browser.

  • Solitaire → Klondike · One player · Draw one

    The classic card game. No account, no downloads, and progress saved on this device.

  • FreeCell → Cards · One player

    Four free cells, eight columns, and room to think.

  • Minesweeper → Puzzle · Two board sizes

    Clear the field. Every number is a clue.

  • 2048 → Puzzle · Swipe or arrow keys

    Slide, merge, and keep doubling.

  • Sudoku → Puzzle · Pencil marks and undo

    Fresh puzzles, each with a unique solution.

  • Spider Solitaire → Cards · One, two, or four suits

    Untangle ten columns into complete sequences.

  • Nonograms → Picture puzzles · 5×5 and 10×10

    Use the clues to uncover six hidden pictures.

  • Snake → Arcade · Three speeds

    A growing appetite and a shrinking margin for error.

  • Reversi → Strategy · Computer or two players

    Flip the board against a local opponent.
